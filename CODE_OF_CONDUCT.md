# Code of Conduct

Voice to Me welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds, abilities and levels of experience. Help keep this community respectful, accessible and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and behavior with patience and constructive feedback.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy and different communication needs.
- Make room for questions and disagreement without personal attacks.
- Accept corrections and help resolve mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, deliberate disruption and spam are not welcome. Do not publish someone else's personal information or private messages without their permission.

## Scope and moderation

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces, and when someone represents Voice to Me in public.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock conversations, restrict participation or block accounts. They will consider the behavior, its impact and any repeated violations. They should explain moderation decisions when appropriate without exposing private information. These expectations also apply to maintainers.

## Reporting

For abuse or harassment on GitHub, use the affected content's **Report content** or **Report abuse** option, or the account's **Block or Report** menu. See [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for the private reporting process and support options.

GitHub handles reports submitted through those platform tools. Repository maintainers handle moderation within project spaces; platform reports are not a private inbox for the maintainers. Avoid opening a public issue with sensitive details about a conduct incident.

Report software vulnerabilities through the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
